Task lifecycle

A task is a thread root with a state on the board. The orchestrator moves it with claims; people move it with answers and the task card. The attempt states inside the orchestrator are on the Orchestrator page.

What the thread shows

  1. The root: the brief, with the task card (state, decision, holder, lease).
  2. attempt 1 started. Session …, branch claude/task-N, routes …
  3. One reply per round: thoughts, actions and outputs, folded; then changes, checks and the worker's answer.
  4. round 2 sent to the worker. when a round follows.
  5. The decision line: PASS, FOLLOWUP, RETRY or REVIEW, and who decided.
  6. draft PR …, merged into main …, task completed; no change was needed., or the reason of a hold or a failure.

Labels on the root: politburo.decision, and on follow-up tasks politburo.followup_of and politburo.followup_depth.
